# Structure of Atom (Unit II: Structure of Atom)
> **Standard:** Class 11 NCERT Chemistry | **Primary Target: NEET**

## 1. Key Principles & Conceptual Summary
Core conceptual framework based on NCERT guidelines for Structure of Atom.

## 2. Canonical Formulas & Constraints
- **bohr_energy**: `$E_n = -\frac{13.6 Z^2}{n^2}\text{ eV}$`
- **bohr_radius**: `$r_n = \frac{a_0 n^2}{Z}$`
- **rydberg**: `$\frac{1}{\lambda}=RZ^2\left(\frac{1}{n_1^2}-\frac{1}{n_2^2}\right)$`

## 3. High-Yield NEET Exam Traps
- ⚠️ NEET: Ionization energy of H = 13.6 eV (remove electron from n=1).
- ⚠️ NEET: Lyman series → UV; Balmer → visible; Paschen → IR.
- ⚠️ NEET: Heisenberg: Δx·Δp ≥ h/(4π) — cannot simultaneously know exact position and momentum.
