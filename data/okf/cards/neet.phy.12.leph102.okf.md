## 2. Canonical Formulas & Constraints
- **potential**: `$V = \frac{kQ}{r}$`
- **capacitance**: `$C = \frac{Q}{V} = \frac{\epsilon_0 A}{d}$`
- **energy_stored**: `$U = \frac{1}{2}CV^2 = \frac{Q^2}{2C}$`

- ⚠️ NEET: Dielectric inserted after disconnecting battery → Q constant, V decreases.
